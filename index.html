Questions

What is this, exactly?

Can I order Gloria today?

We’re preparing the US launch and TikTok Shop listings for these three Android models. We’ll open orders once pricing, service terms, inventory and delivery details are confirmed. This website does not currently take payments or collect signups.

Is the Android hardware the same in every size?

No. The 6-inch model has 2 GB RAM, dual microphones and no camera. The 10.1-inch model has 4 GB RAM, a single microphone and a 5 MP camera. The 21.5-inch has 4 GB RAM, a four-microphone array and an 8 MP camera. All three have 32 GB storage.

Does Gloria need an internet connection or a subscription?

An internet connection is required for the AI service. The hardware purchase price, service pricing and included usage will be shown before orders open. Android hardware by itself does not include unlimited AI service.

Will the small model look exactly like the image?

The image presents the 21.5-inch model using the approved reference-based visualization. The 6-inch and 10.1-inch units have different dimensions. Model-specific product photos will accompany the final sales listings.

Does Gloria need access to my email and calendar?

No. Conversation is the starting point. Optional integrations are part of the product direction, and their availability will be confirmed with the service offering.
