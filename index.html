The idea

A tab has no presence.

Gloria gives your assistant a place in the room. Not another app to open. Something you can glance at, speak to and progressively trust with more of your day.

The hologram is the hook. Presence is the product.

Questions

What is this, exactly?

Is Gloria already a finished product?
Not yet. This is an early-access concept built around a working hologram prototype and an evolving personal-assistant stack. Joining tells us you want to be part of the first group we build for.
Does Gloria need access to everything?
No. Start as a conversational assistant, then connect specific services only when they become useful.
Why a hologram?
Because an assistant that has a place in the room feels different from another icon on your phone. The point isn’t spectacle. It’s presence.
What happens when Gloria wants to send or change something?
Sensitive actions are designed to require clear confirmation. The goal is useful autonomy without invisible authority.
