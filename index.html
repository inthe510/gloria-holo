Questions

What is this, exactly?

What does Gloria remember?

Gloria is designed to carry meaningful context between conversations, including preferences, routines and goals you share. That does not mean perfect or unlimited recall. Memory settings, retention and controls will be explained with the launch service.

Can I order Gloria today?

We’re preparing our own online store for these three Android models, shipping from California. Prices are shown in the plan comparison. Orders will open once usage allowances, contract terms, inventory and delivery details are confirmed. This website does not currently take payments or collect signups.

Is the Android hardware the same in every size?

No. The 6-inch model has 2 GB RAM, dual microphones and no camera. The 10.1-inch model has 4 GB RAM, a single microphone and a 5 MP camera. The 21.5-inch has 4 GB RAM, a four-microphone array and an 8 MP camera. All three have 32 GB storage.

Does Gloria need an internet connection or a subscription?

An internet connection is required for the AI service. In addition to the upfront device payment, Companion is $79/month and Assistant is $199/month, each with a 12-month commitment. Shipping and tax are additional. Usage allowances will be detailed before orders open; unlimited usage is not promised.

Will the small model look exactly like the image?

The image presents the 21.5-inch model using the approved reference-based visualization. The 6-inch and 10.1-inch units have different dimensions. Model-specific product photos will accompany the final sales listings.

Does Gloria need access to my email and calendar?

No. You can start with conversation. Email and calendar assistance are Assistant-plan features and use optional account connections and your permission; supported services will be listed before purchase.
